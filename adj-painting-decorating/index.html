About ADJ Painting

Decorating runs in the family

Darren started working as a self-employed painter & decorator in November 2021, after over 20 years in sales. Early in his career he worked for decorating firms — including the 999-call centre, Newmarket racecourse and a Makro store — and decorating runs in his family.

ADJ stands for After Dry January — the name of a group Darren set up on Facebook just over 5 years ago. Setting up ADJ Painting & Decorating was a by-product of quitting drinking alcohol. Best thing he did — and his reviews speak for themselves.
