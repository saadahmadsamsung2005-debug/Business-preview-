Built for

01

Arbroath Muay Thai trains inside Breaking Point Gym in Arbroath — a proper combat-sports studio with matted floors and punch bags, run for amateur and professional training alike.

Led by experienced coach Denise, the club teaches authentic Thai boxing alongside practical self-protection — for anyone aged 16 and over, whatever your fitness or experience.

Everything you need is here. The club can supply you with all the equipment you need — just bring yourself.

02 — Training

What you
train.

Three ways in. Every session is coached, structured and built around real Thai boxing — not a fitness class dressed up as one.

01

Thai Boxing

The art of eight limbs. Punches, kicks, knees, elbows and clinch work — drilled on pads and bags until the technique is yours. Fitness, timing and confidence come with it.

Ages 16+ · All levels
02

Self-Protection

Practical self-defence for the real world. Awareness, distance, and simple, effective techniques you can actually use — taught in a safe, respectful environment.
