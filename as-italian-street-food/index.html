About AS Italian Street Food

Twenty years of proper Italian cooking

AS Italian Street Food is the food-truck arm of Puntino Catering & Events, run by Antonio Sabia — former owner of Puntino Restaurant in Woolloomooloo for 20 years, and a festival food vendor for over a decade.

We are a pre-approved mobile food vendor with the City of Canada Bay. Book the truck for your festival, event or private party, and we bring the food to you.

  • Woodfired pizza, made to order
  • Arrosticini & gnocchi — proper Italian
  • Festivals, events & private parties
  • Pre-approved City of Canada Bay vendor
