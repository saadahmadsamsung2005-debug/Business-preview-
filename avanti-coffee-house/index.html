About Avanti

Ilkley’s first independent coffee house

Avanti Coffee Co. prides itself on excellent food, drink and customer service, given in a friendly and relaxed atmosphere. We were Ilkley’s first independent coffee house — and we still do things the independent way.

Everything is made with care: hand-roasted coffee, homemade cakes, and food cooked fresh each day. Come and see us on The Grove Promenade, right in the heart of Ilkley.
