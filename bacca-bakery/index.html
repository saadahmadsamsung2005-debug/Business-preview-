From field to oven — one of the shortest journeys in New Zealand.

Bacca works closely with local farmers and producers to revive grain-growing in Golden Bay. Some of the wheat in your loaf is grown just kilometres from the oven, then stoneground and slowly fermented over several days for depth, flavour and digestibility.

Everything is made from scratch with organic ingredients — from sourdough loaves and rye brownies to laminated pastries and savoury pies. Find us at the bakery, at Tākaka Saturday Market, and at Golden Bay Organics.
