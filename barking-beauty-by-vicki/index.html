About Barking Beauty

Home-run, fully insured — and loved locally

Barking Beauty is run by Vicki McClelland/Jameson from her home in Craigavon, with over 14 years in the dog grooming and care industry. All breeds and sizes of dogs are welcome, and every dog is catered for individually.

Dogs are only kept in our care for the length of their grooming procedure, which greatly reduces the stress of being away from home — so they leave wagging their tails.
