About Barks & Bubbles

New salon, deep experience

Barks & Bubbles opened in Ashton-under-Lyne in 2025, led by trainer Joanna Anderson. The team’s fully qualified groomers bring 12 years of experience, and the salon also teaches the art of professional grooming.

We specialise in all breeds and groom to the highest standards, meeting every owner’s requirements — and we’ve been nominated for the 2026 High Achievement Awards.
