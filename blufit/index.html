Meet Kelly Blue

Kelly Blue is a certified personal trainer and the founder of Blufit. She opened the studio in 2025 to give the women of Inverclyde something Greenock didn’t have — a private, female-only gym where you can train hard, feel safe, and actually enjoy it.

From first-timers who have never touched a barbell to experienced lifters chasing a new PB, Kelly coaches every woman like she matters — because here, she does.

  • Certified personal trainer & online coach
  • Female-only, private studio — no crowded gym floor
  • Small classes, limited numbers, real attention
  • Community events — including the Blufit Games
