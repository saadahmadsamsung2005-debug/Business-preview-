About Butter & Crumb

A little cottage bakery with a big heart

Butter & Crumb Bakery is a micro cottage bakery in Gresham, Oregon, owned and operated by Koryn. Every bake is small-batch and handcrafted — made with love, one tray at a time.

We sell at local artisan fairs — most recently the Italia Holiday Artisan Fair at Club Paesano in Gresham — and take pre-orders on Facebook. Order ahead, and pick up fresh.
