02 / THE CLUB

More than a gym

Northfield's boxing family since 2010.

Byron is a community amateur club, not a commercial gym. Over 50 members, aged 8 to 60, train at Northfield Community Centre — and the club has produced a string of Scottish champions along the way.

In 2023, 13-year-old Byron boxer Michael Kahl won silver at the European School Championships in Slovenia. The gym also produced undefeated super-middleweight professional John Docherty — proof of what Northfield coaching can do.
