Our story

Family-run since 1964

Christine's Dog Grooming Parlour has been a family business in Edinburgh since 1964. Generations of local dogs — and their owners — have trusted us with their regular trims, baths and tidy-ups.

Bookings have always been made by phone or Facebook. This page gives customers a simple way to request an appointment online.
