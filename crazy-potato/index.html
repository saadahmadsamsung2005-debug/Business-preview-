About Crazy Potato

A festival favourite, run by Sharrie

Crazy Potato is a mobile food truck run by Sharrie Talanoa, trading at festivals and events across Sydney — and Canberra, where we are booked for Enlighten Festival 2026.

Everything is made fresh on the spot, and our twisted potato is gluten-free. Book the truck for your event, or just come and find us at the next festival.
