01 — THE STUDIO

Lincoln ink,
done properly.

Eternal Tattoos has been on Wragby Road since 2010 — fifteen years of custom work, repeat clients and word-of-mouth that keeps the diary full.

Every tattoo starts as a conversation, not a catalogue. Bring your idea — a photo, a sketch, a half-formed thought — and the artist draws it up with you until it is yours alone.

Clean, calm and professional: a licensed studio where hygiene comes first, aftercare is explained properly, and nobody is rushed into a decision they will wear for life.

  • Custom designs drawn for each client — no two pieces the same
  • Free consultation before you commit to anything
  • Cover-ups and reworks assessed honestly, in person
  • Walk-ins welcome when the diary allows
  • Fully licensed, hygienic studio — aftercare guidance included
