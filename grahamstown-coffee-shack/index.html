Ten years on Pollen Street

A little shack with a big heart

Grahamstown is the quirky historic precinct at the northern end of Pollen Street — antiques, galleries, the market, and us.

For ten years we've been pouring coffee, baking pies and feeding Thames — neighbours, travellers on the Coromandel road, and everyone who pops in for "just a coffee" and stays for lunch.
