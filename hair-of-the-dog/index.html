03 / ABOUT
A salon built on patience, not speed.

Helene & Andy have run Hair of the Dog in Carlton since February 2017. What started as a love of dogs became Nottingham's go-to salon for nervous and anxious dogs — a happy, stress-free place where every dog is listened to.

Our logo is a Wire Fox Terrier, inspired by Mr Schmoo — the little dog Helené rehomed during her training. He still watches over the salon today.

We always listen to what you would like your dog to look like, and we groom every breed — from a first puppy pamper to a full groom for the most nervous rescue.
