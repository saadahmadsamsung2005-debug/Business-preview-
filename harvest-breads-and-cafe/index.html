About Harvest Breads

An artisan bakery-cafe in the heart of Maroochydore

Harvest Breads & Cafe is an artisan bakery-cafe at the Kon Tiki Building in Maroochydore. Everything is baked in-house — from authentic sourdough loaves to baked cheesecake — and the cafe serves breakfast and lunch all week.

We also cater — from morning teas to a full catering menu — and sell our own branded coffee beans and fresh bread to take home.
