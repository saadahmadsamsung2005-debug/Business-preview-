Why Highland Top Team

More than a martial arts gym. A family on the mats.

We’re a dedicated Jiu-Jitsu academy — not a bit of everything. That focus is why families across the Highlands choose us, from a child’s first class to an adult’s first competition.

1

BJJ only — and proud of it

One art, taught properly, every day. No crowded multi-sport timetable where Jiu-Jitsu is an afterthought.

2

Black-belt led

Head coach Aiden MacKenzie is a black belt, and every class is coached — you’re never left to figure it out alone.

3

Family first

Kids, teens and adults train under one roof. Parents watch their children grow in confidence while training themselves.

4

Competition-proven

Our team competes across Scotland and the UK — and brings the medals home.
