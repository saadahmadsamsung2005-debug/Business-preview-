About Hodson's Hounds

Small shop, big standards

Hodson’s Hounds has been looking after Blackburn’s dogs for 10 years. We are a small shop offering all grooming needs on a one-to-one basis, with fully qualified staff and insurance.

We use natural shampoos and products matched to your pet’s coat, and we offer a pick-up and drop-off service — professional, friendly service at reasonable rates.
