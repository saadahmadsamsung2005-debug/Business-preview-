02 — WHY HYBRID

Not a Big-Box Gym.
A Training Crew.

Westhill has big leisure centres and Aberdeen has big commercial gyms. Hybrid Transform is the opposite: small, coached, and built around the athlete in front of the coach.

Sample photo

01 / Coached, Always

Every class has a coach on the floor — not a timetable on a wall. Technique is taught, watched and corrected in every session.

02 / Small Groups, Big Attention

Small-group training means the coach actually sees you — your form, your effort, your progress — and pushes you at the right level.

03 / Built for Young Athletes

GymFit, BoxFit and HYROX Youngstars are age-appropriate by design: strength, speed and confidence, built the right way from the start.

04 / Right Here in Westhill

Training at Lawsondale Playing Fields — no trip into Aberdeen, no anonymous gym floor. Local training, for local families.
