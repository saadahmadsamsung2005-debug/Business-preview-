01 — The Studio

Small studio.
Serious coaching.

KMB Fitness & Rehabilitation is an independent personal-training studio at 38 Bloomgate, Lanark, run by Kristi Barr. No crowded gym floor, no waiting for equipment — just focused coaching in a private, supportive space.

Whether you are coming back from a sports injury, starting training for the first time, or looking for a coach who actually watches every rep, your programme is built around you — your body, your goals, your pace.

One-to-one personal training, tailored to your goals and your starting point.
Sports-injury rehabilitation — progressive strength and mobility work to get you moving well again.
Group classes for training together, with a coach who still sees every person in the room.
Welcoming to every age — from teens building healthy habits to seniors staying strong and mobile.
