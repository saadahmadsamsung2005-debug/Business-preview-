01 — The Studio

A gym that feels
like yours.

Know Your Strength is a boutique strength gym at Caldwellside Industrial Estate — a small, private and inclusive space for people to train, or learn to train, without the usual gym stigmas.

Coaching here is education-led: you learn how your body moves, how to lift safely, and how to build strength that lasts — with a strong focus on women's health, confidence and long-term, real-life strength.

Booking system — a maximum of 8 people per hour, so it is never overcrowded.
Private personal-training and small class-based studio, coached by Kerrie & Caitlin.
Welcoming to complete beginners — especially anyone who has ever felt scared of gyms.
