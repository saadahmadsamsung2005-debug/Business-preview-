A small shop with a big reputation

Kim and Ra Sok started Lunchbar Eighty8 a few years ago, selling the usual staples — mince and cheese, mac and cheese. Then they started experimenting with something no one else was offering: seafood pies.

Now people come from Australia and America just to try the pāua pie — and the Waikato Times called it a creamy pie sensation that draws fans from across the globe.
