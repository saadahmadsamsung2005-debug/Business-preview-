The Club
in Dumfries

Nith Valley Amateur Boxing Club trains at the Lesser Loreburn Hall, Newall Terrace — the same hall where Head Coach Mark Dawson first learned to box as a 17-year-old with Dumfries Amateur Boxing Club back in 1982.

Mark founded Nith Valley in 2009, and the club has since coached hundreds of young people from across Dumfries & Galloway — from first-timers finding their feet to boxers winning medals at shows across Scotland and beyond.

It is a community club in the truest sense: home shows at Loreburn Hall, families in the crowd, and a gym where juniors, ladies and seniors all train under the same roof.

Your Coach
Mark Dawson

Mark Dawson started boxing at Loreburn Hall himself as a teenager in 1982. More than forty years later he is still there — now as owner and Head Coach of the club he founded.

Under Mark, Nith Valley boxers have boxed — and won — at shows across Scotland, including Western District novice shows and Box Cups further afield. His teams keep coming home with medals.

“You could say we are a club of winners — that’s what we seem to do.”— Mark Dawson, Head Coach, on his Nith Valley boxers

Newcomers are welcome at any session. Call Mark first if you’d like to check anything — or just come along to Loreburn Hall.
