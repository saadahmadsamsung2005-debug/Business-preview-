A touch of Dutch, baked in Waipū

Small bakery, big heart

Petra's Pastries is a local mobile bakery in Waipū — homemade baked goods, made with care and brought to the community, week after week.

There's no shopfront and no fuss: just good bread and pastries, a table at the market, and regulars who know to come early.

Petra also bakes for local events — if you'd like her pastries at your gathering, market or celebration, just ask.
