About Punisher

A community gym,
not a fight club

Punisher Kickboxing is a community kickboxing school in Edinburgh. Our experienced instructors coach kids, adults and women of all fitness levels — from your very first class to regular training.

We train hard, but the room is welcoming. You will learn real kickboxing technique, get fitter, and pick up the values the sport is built on: discipline, respect and self-confidence — on the mats and off them.
