03 — About the parlour

A parlour that treats every dog like its own.

Puppy Love Dog Parlour is a small, caring grooming salon in the heart of Lisburn. Every groom is one-to-one, so your dog gets the groomer’s full attention from start to finish — groomed by Julie and the team of certified groomers, at your dog’s pace.

From a first puppy groom to a full breed cut, the salon works with your dog to give one of the most positive grooming experiences possible — a tailored style that suits you and your pet.

  • One-to-one grooming, full attention
  • No cages — calm and unhurried
  • Certified, experienced groomers
  • All breeds welcome
