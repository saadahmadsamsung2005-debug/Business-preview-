02 — Services

Three crafts.
Done properly.

Every service is one-on-one in the private suite. Pick one below — it pre-selects in the booking form.

N° 01

Precision Cut

A clean, considered cut — shaped to you, finished with detail. No conveyor belt, no ten-minute rush.

N° 02

Skin Fade

Sharp lines, smooth blend, zero harshness. The fade is built up slowly until it sits exactly right.

Beard Work & Line-Up

Beard shaped, lined and finished with a straight razor — the detail that makes the whole cut look sharp.
