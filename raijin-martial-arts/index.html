02 — THE ACADEMY

More Than a Club.
A Way of Life.

Raijin Martial Arts Academy is dedicated to providing people with life skills that can be used not only in their sport, but in everyday life.

We teach sport karate and kickboxing at our full-time studio in the heart of Inverness, with classes for all ages, all fitness levels and all experiences in martial arts. Our teachers are friendly, patient and welcoming — especially with the kids.

Raijin — 雷神 — is the Japanese god of thunder. That is the spirit we train with: power with control, speed with discipline, and respect in everything we do.
