A family bakery at the heart of Westport.

Rainbow Cake Kitchen has fed Westport for generations — birthdays, morning teas, school lunches and Sunday treats. We're a family-run bakery, and we still bake the way we always have: fresh every day, with local milk and cream from a grass-fed organic dairy.
