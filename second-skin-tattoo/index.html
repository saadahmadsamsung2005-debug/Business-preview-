Twenty-six years.
One standard.

Not a trend-chasing shop. A craft studio that has earned its reputation one tattoo at a time, since 1998.

Second Skin Tattoo has served Immingham and North East Lincolnshire since 1998 — custom tattoos designed around you, in a studio that puts safety and honesty first.

Every piece starts with a free design and consultation. Bring your idea, a photo, or just a feeling — Jim and Didz will draw it with you, size it with you, and only start when you are completely happy with the design. The studio is registered with the local health authority and works to medical-grade hygiene standards, so you can relax in the chair.

No website, no online booking, no portfolio page — until now, everything has run on reputation, phone calls and Facebook messages. This website changes that: see the work, pick your style, and book your free consultation in a tap.
