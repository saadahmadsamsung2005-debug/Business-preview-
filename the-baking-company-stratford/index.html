A 1920s building, a Stratford local

Set on the corner of Regan Street, opposite the Z station, in an old 1920s brick building painted green and red — once a doctor's surgery, now a bakery. With Mt Taranaki on the horizon and plenty of parking, it is an easy stop on the way through town.
