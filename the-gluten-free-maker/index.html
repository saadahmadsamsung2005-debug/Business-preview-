Baked by a chef, not a factory

Vicki trained as a chef in Sydney 40 years ago and has cooked here and overseas ever since — running renowned restaurants including Bayswater Café in Napier and Bolderson's in Hastings, and creating one of Aotearoa's first artisan bakeries.

When she began baking gluten-free at home, she spent five months perfecting her own flour blends — brown rice, white rice, tapioca, potato starch, buckwheat and corn — so nothing tastes gummy or gritty. Every box is still made entirely by hand in her Hawke's Bay kitchen.
