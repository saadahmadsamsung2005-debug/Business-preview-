From home kitchen to high street

A lockdown hobby that grew up

Tinapay Atbp began as a home-based hobby during the COVID-19 lockdowns — baking Filipino breads for family, friends and neighbours who missed a taste of home.

It grew through online sales and markets, became a Limited Company in March 2025, and opened its first retail store at 406 Don Buck Road, Massey in August 2025 — Tinapay Atbp at The Filipino Corner.

Today the bakery serves its neighbourhood daily, supplies wholesale customers and caters Filipino food for gatherings — still made fresh, with love, and with quality ingredients.
