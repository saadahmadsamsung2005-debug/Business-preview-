About Tulip & Grape

Janice & Victoria

Tulip & Grape is a mother-and-daughter wedding décor and floristry business in Newtownards, founded in 2023. What began as a shared love of beautiful weddings has grown into a hire catalogue full of the pieces couples ask for most.

With a wide range of ornaments and furnishings — and a strong sense of trends and themes — we will bring your vision to life, from an intimate elopement to a wedding of sixty guests.

  • Floristry for elopements & weddings up to 60 guests
  • Hire catalogue — vases, candlesticks, easels & more
  • Flat £15 delivery & collection across Northern Ireland
  • Please book at least 3 months in advance

— Janice & Victoria, Tulip & Grape
