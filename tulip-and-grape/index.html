About Tulip & Grape

It started with Victoria’s own wedding

Tulip & Grape is owned by the close mother-and-daughter duo, Janice and Victoria. Victoria’s marriage in 2023 sparked the idea — she noticed a big gap in the market in Northern Ireland, and together they built this unique service.

With a wide range of ornaments and furnishings, and a strong sense of trends and themes, we will bring your vision to life. Please book at least 3 months in advance.
