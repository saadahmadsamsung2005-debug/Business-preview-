A Tūrangi favourite

Right where the adventure starts

Tūrangi sits at the southern end of Lake Taupō, on the doorstep of the Tongariro — trout fishing, the Tongariro Alpine Crossing and the river trail all begin here.

Our bakehouse and café is right in the middle of town, so whether you're fuelling up for a big day out or winding down after one, there's a warm welcome, a full cabinet and a hot coffee waiting.
