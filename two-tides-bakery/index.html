Part of the community

Two Tides is owned and run by Stacey Buchanan and Jenniffer Kagaoan, with a multicultural team who make sure everyone who walks in feels the good vibes — quirky signs, a photo wall, and plenty of laughter.

The bakery was a finalist and runner-up in the Hospitality category of the Hauraki–Coromandel Business Awards, and took part in the Coromandel Goodness sustainability programme.
